Rare reward proposal

A long run without a rare reward can leave a player with little sense of progress. This proposal introduces a while retaining a chance-based reward.

1Increase the chance after repeated missesProposed

Current situation: Every eligible encounter uses the same 10% , regardless of the player's previous results.

Problem: Repeated misses provide no progress toward the next rare reward.

Proposed change: After five consecutive misses, increase the chance by five percentage points after each further miss, up to 100%. Reset the counter after every encounter. Revised January 2: reset only when a rare reward is received.

Player impact: A long sequence without a rare reward becomes less likely, while early rewards remain uncertain.

Cost: Store the counter, retest the reward distribution, and assess the effect on progression speed.

2Open questionsOpen

Should the counter carry across sessions?

Keeping the counter would preserve progress when a player leaves. Resetting it would make the result depend on the length of a session.

Annotation

Should the current chance be visible?

Showing the chance would explain the player's progress, but would also require an additional element in the reward interface.
